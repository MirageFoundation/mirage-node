# Mirage v1.16.0 Release Notes

### Agent System
Moderators have been reimagined as Agents, giving you a clearer way to choose whose curation you trust. Enabling an agent lets their moderation and voting signals shape your feed, so your experience reflects the people you rely on rather than a one-size-fits-all default.

### Simpler, Clearer Tiers
Subscriptions are now a straightforward three-tier lineup: Free, Subscriber, and Agent. Subscriber stays at 100,000 MIRAGE per month for personalization and higher limits, while Agent is 500,000 MIRAGE per month and unlocks the ability to become an agent yourself along with extra profile flair.

### Stronger Network Reserve
To keep relay fees stable and the network resilient, 95% of each subscription now flows into the network reserve. The tradeoff is less token burn, but the benefit is a stronger buffer for long-term sustainability and smoother fee handling.

### Upgrade Notes
This is a breaking upgrade and nodes must move to v1.16.0 to stay in sync. Your existing account and history carry forward, and tier mappings plus agent lists are migrated automatically during the upgrade.
