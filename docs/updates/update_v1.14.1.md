# Mirage v1.14.1 Release Notes

**Account deletion**: v1.14 brings account deletion to Mirage. Your data, your choice — head to Settings, type "DELETE," and your entire presence is wiped from the chain: profile, username, follows, blocks, subscription, everything. Your username is freed up instantly for anyone else to claim, and any remaining token balance flows back to the community pool.

**Blockchain transparency**: Mirage is built on a blockchain, so deletion works a bit differently than what you're used to. When you delete your account, the network broadcasts a deletion request to every node. The vast majority will honor it immediately, but because each node is independently operated, we can't promise that every last copy disappears from every corner of the network. Your historical posts stick around with your old username for context — but your profile and identity are gone for good.

**Governance deletion**: For cases where the community needs to step in, account deletion can also happen through governance. If a spam account, an impersonator, or a bad actor needs to go, the community can propose and vote on it — no user signature needed. When the vote passes, the same full cleanup runs automatically.

**Magic sort**: Your home feed now runs on Magic — a new sort mode that replaces the old chronological default. Magic scores every post with a formula that blends four signals: how much similar users liked it, its net votes, how many unique people joined the conversation, and your personal taste in topics and authors. A recency curve keeps things fresh without burying quality posts the moment they age past an hour. Every post in your feed shows a one-line reason — "Similar users liked this," "Active discussion," "You like #topic" — so you always know *why* something showed up. If you'd rather just scroll the timeline, flip to **Newest** with the toggle at the top of your feed.

**Magic for everyone**: Magic works for everyone, not just power users. Guests get the same scoring minus personalization — popular and actively discussed posts float to the top out of the box. Topic feeds and the Following feed use the same scorer with preferences dialed to zero, so the ranking feels consistent everywhere you browse. The algorithm runs server-side on every request, so there's nothing to train and no "algorithm settings" to fiddle with — it just works.

**Rate limiting**: We've also tightened up the infrastructure in this release. All API and chain endpoints now enforce per-IP rate limiting — 10 requests per second on API routes, 5 on chain routes — with clean JSON errors instead of cryptic HTML when you hit the ceiling. Structured access logs, automatic Cloudflare IP trust, and smarter cache headers round out the hardening.

**How deletion actually works**: When you delete your account, the chain marks your profile as deleted and wipes your data from the official state — profile, username, follows, blocks, subscription. Your local app clears your seed phrase and logs you out. But here's the honest part: Mirage is a decentralized network, and each node operator runs their own copy. The deletion message goes out to every node, and the default software honors it, but a node operator *can* choose to ignore it or run modified software that keeps the data. Think of it as "marked for deletion" across the network — the vast majority of nodes will comply, but we can't force every last one. That's the tradeoff of decentralization.

**Security**: The deletion flow is locked down where it matters: the chain rejects any attempt to delete someone else's account, the API enforces the same ownership check, and the full self-delete path is validated end to end with dedicated tests. No one can delete your account but you (or a governance vote).
