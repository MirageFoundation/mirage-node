# Mirage v1.7.0 Release Notes

### Overview

Good platforms grow organically. Great platforms reward the people who make that happen. With v1.7.0, Mirage introduces **Invite & Earn**: bring someone to the conversation, and when they participate, you earn MIRAGE tokens. It's that simple.

---

### How It Works

Share your personal referral link. When someone signs up through that link and starts posting, you earn **1 MIRAGE for each day they're active**. But it doesn't stop there.

If your referral invites someone else, you earn from their activity too. And it goes **five levels deep**: your direct invite earns you the full rate, their invites earn you half, and it keeps halving down to level five. One person you invite today could generate rewards for years as their network grows beneath them.

Build a network of active participants and watch your earnings compound.

---

### Real Activity, Real Rewards

This isn't a referral link you spam and forget. The system tracks genuine activity. Your invite actually has to show up, post something real, engage with the community. Gaming attempts are detected and blocked by AI-powered fraud detection. We built this to reward authentic growth, not manufactured numbers.

Each referred user can earn you rewards for up to **10 active days**, ensuring the system stays sustainable while still offering meaningful incentives. Quality over quantity.

---

### Your Referral Dashboard

Your referral tree shows exactly who you've invited and what you've earned:

- See your full network visualized across all five levels
- Track pending rewards in real-time
- Watch your earnings grow as your network expands
- Rewards accrue automatically at midnight UTC

---

### Get Started

1. Go to the **Invite & Earn** page
2. Copy your personal referral link
3. Share it with people who would genuinely enjoy Mirage
4. Earn MIRAGE when they participate

The best communities are built by their members. Now you get rewarded for building ours.
