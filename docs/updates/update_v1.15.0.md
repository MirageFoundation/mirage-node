# Mirage v1.15.0 Release Notes

### Burn-only awards

You can now give awards to posts and comments. Awards are a pure signal of appreciation, not a payout — the MIRAGE cost is permanently burned rather than sent to the creator. There are four awards: Quality Post costs 10,000 MIRAGE, while Original Content, Based AF, and Receipts cost 5,000 MIRAGE each.

### Recognition with guardrails

Each account can give one award per post or comment, and you can never award your own content. When multiple people give the same award, it stacks into a single count, and when different awards appear together they display as a tidy list so recognition stays readable instead of noisy.

### Admin recognition

Admins (level 100 and above) can give any award for free. This gives moderators a way to spotlight great work without turning recognition into a personal expense.

### Community-tuned awards

Award types and costs are governed by the community. The network can add new awards, remove old ones, or adjust costs through governance without waiting for another code release.

### Smarter Magic feed

Awards now feed into the Magic ranking model by counting how many distinct accounts recognized a post. Content that earns broad recognition from different users gets a clear visibility boost alongside votes, discussion, and personal relevance.
